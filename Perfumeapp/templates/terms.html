Terms & Conditions

Perfume is a beautiful art form Authenticity: Perfumes sold must be genuine and not counterfeit. Retailers often guarantee authenticity. Usage Disclaimer: Perfume is for external use only. Brands usually warn against ingestion or applying to broken skin. Allergy & Sensitivity: Companies disclaim responsibility for allergic reactions; customers are advised to test before full use. Returns & Refunds: Many stores only accept returns if the perfume is unopened and unused, due to hygiene reasons. Shelf Life: Perfumes generally have a shelf life (often 3–5 years). Terms may state that expired products are not covered. Liability: Brands limit liability for misuse (e.g., spraying near eyes, flames, or sensitive areas). Intellectual Property: Perfume names, bottle designs, and logos are trademarked; copying or resale without authorization may be restricted. Shipping Restrictions: Perfumes are flammable, so terms often specify shipping limitations (e.g., no air freight to certain regions).
